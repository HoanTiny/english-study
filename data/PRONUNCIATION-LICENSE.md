# Attribution for the learner IPA dataset

`src/data/vocabulary-pronunciation.json` and its compact derivative
`src/data/vocabulary-ipa.json` contain adapted English pronunciation transcriptions
from Wiktionary contributors, plus editorial corrections and dictionary citation
forms. They are shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The full JSON retains source links, including pinned Wiktionary revision IDs,
for each word/accent. Editorial changes normalize notation, choose the sense
used in the Vietnamese course, correct stress or accent, and supply word-by-word
transcriptions where a phrase has no complete entry. An editorial method label
does not mean its exact transcription was quoted from the linked page.

The original PDF transcriptions remain in the original import and `sourceIpa`;
they are not represented as verified US/UK IPA. Ten unverified proper names are
explicitly held without a guessed English transcription.

Audio has separate per-file licensing. The `word_audio.attribution` object
retains the recording creator, Commons source, and license URL. Those credits
are shown in the lesson. Do not replace them with this dataset license.

These data licenses do not change the license of application source code.
